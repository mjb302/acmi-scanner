Five steps, under a minute

Click a step to see it on the phone.

Scan

Camera, a saved photo, or paste text. Text recognition runs on the device.

ACMI

Score, category and a one-line summary for the notes. Brand matches flagged for you to confirm.

2

Refine

Optional clinical context tick boxes and an end-of-life mode that separates symptom-control medicines.

3

STOPP

STOPP v3 criteria for each contributing drug, collapsed per medicine, with a tick box to consider deprescribing.

4

Plan

Taper, cautions and monitoring prompts, ACMI before and after, and a downloadable report.

What you get in the notes

One line, computed in code, never by a language model.

ACMI: morphine 1.5 + oxybutynin 1.2 + furosemide 1.1 + amitriptyline 1.0 = 4.8 (High)

88-drug ACMI

Generic matching with UK brand and combination-product mapping. Each ingredient scores once; PRN only if every entry is PRN. Topical preparations and Buscopan are never scored.

Reads real lists

Uses only the current/repeat section when an acute section is present, ignores stopped items, and merges direction lines with the medicine above.

STOPP v3 built in

Criterion text verbatim from O'Mahony et al. 2023. List-level rules (M1, A3, L2, L3) and route-dependent advice for inhaled versus systemic glycopyrronium.

End-of-life mode

Mark symptom-control medicines; their criteria collapse and everything else is flagged for review, rather than hiding all prompts.

Deprescribing plan

Generic taper, cautions and "restart if" prompts per drug class, with drug-specific notes such as furosemide in heart failure.

Private by design

No account, no network calls, no stored photos or text. Only anonymous usage counters, which you choose whether to share.
